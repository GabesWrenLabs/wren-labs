Do you drive out to check a stock tank?

We are a small electronics shop in Utah. We're designing a box that sits at a remote tank and tells you at the house when the water drops. Radio link, no cell signal needed at the tank, no monthly fee.

We haven't built it yet, and we're not selling anything today. Before we build, we want to hear from people who actually water cattle from tanks they can't see from the house. Ten minutes of your experience is worth more to us than a month of guessing.

Three things we'd like to know

  1. How far is your farthest tank, and how often does someone drive out to look at it?
  2. Last time a tank ran dry or ran over: what did it cost you? Head, hours, fuel, a float, a pump?
  3. Cell signal at the tank? Any power nearby (solar pump, fence charger)?
