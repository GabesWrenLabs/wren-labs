What you get

BaseEthernet (Wi-Fi fallback), USB-C powered. Talks to ATEM over the network (so the ATEM Mini works, no tally port needed), to vMix over its TCP tally API, and to OBS over obs-websocket. Two screw terminals for a plain cue switch, for theatres with no switcher at all.
4 lightsBright red = program, green = preview, off = idle. Brightness button. USB-C powered, with an optional internal battery for rigs with no power. 1/4"-20 thread, cold-shoe adapter and a velcro strip in the box.
SetupLights arrive paired to their base. Open the base's setup page on any phone, type the switcher's IP, assign light 1-4 to camera 1-4. Settings stick, so the volunteer who runs it next week touches nothing.
RangeAcross a sanctuary or a stage. Target 50 m / 150 ft indoors. We'll publish the measured number from the prototype.

Why we're building it

We read six years of forum threads from church stream volunteers and stage managers asking for the same thing: a few wireless tally or cue lights for a couple hundred dollars that don't need an app, a programmer or a weekly charging ritual. The kits on sale cost $500 to $700, need a GPI box for the ATEM Mini, or make you flash an M5Stick yourself. We think that's fixable.
